# DEFECT — motte-and-bailey (target axis: **Equivocation**)

> This is an **openly-constructed adversarial probe**, not a real artifact. The construction
> *is* the ground truth: the defect below was engineered in deliberately. It does not stand in
> for anyone's real claim. (See `../README.md` for why that is not a no-fabrication violation.)

## The engineered defect

A **motte-and-bailey**: a single key term, *intelligent*, equivocates between two senses.

- **Bailey** (the controversial claim actually being asserted): the recommendation engine is
  *genuinely intelligent* — possesses understanding in the strong, mind-like sense.
- **Motte** (the trivially defensible position retreated to under fire): it "learns from feedback
  and adapts its output" — true of essentially any machine-learning system, and of a thermostat.

The final clause performs the retreat in advance: *"once you accept that a system learns from
feedback and adapts its output, you've already granted it's intelligent in the only sense that has
ever mattered."* This asserts the two senses are one — it smuggles the bailey in on the motte's
credentials. The word *intelligent* carries the whole move.

**This is the Equivocation axis by construction:** *"does a key term shift meaning or hide behind a
buzzword?"* Yes — *intelligent* shifts from strong-sense cognition to weak-sense adaptivity within
one sentence.

## Axes it is deliberately clean on

- **Evidence** — no empirical magnitude or data is staked that would need citation; "learns from
  every interaction" is true of the system and not in dispute.
- **Base rate / magnitude** — no quantity is claimed.
- **Causality vs correlation** — no cause is inferred from an association.
- **Falsifiability** — "learns and adapts" is observable and checkable; the claim is not vacuous in
  the falsifiability sense (its defect is term-shift, not untestability).

## Most likely *adjacent* catches (not the target, but acceptable signal)

- **Counterexample** — a thermostat learns and adapts yet is not intelligent; the critic may fire
  here instead of, or alongside, Equivocation. An adjacent catch still surfaces the defect.
- **Hidden premise** — the unstated premise "adaptivity = intelligence" is exactly the equivocation
  viewed from another angle; a hidden-premise finding is the same catch under a different axis name.

A flag on Equivocation, Counterexample, or Hidden-premise all count as the probe *working*. A
verdict of `substantive` with **no** finding touching the term-shift is a **false pass**.

## How this probe was constructed

Took the canonical motte-and-bailey structure (defend the easy position, operate on the hard one)
and bound it to one polysemous word common in product copy, *intelligent*, so the equivocation is
the single salient defect. Rhetorical flourishes that would trip other axes (claimed statistics,
causal mechanism, magnitude comparisons) were deliberately omitted to isolate the signal.
