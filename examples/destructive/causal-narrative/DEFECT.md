# DEFECT — causality from correlation (target axis: **Causality vs correlation**)

> Openly-constructed adversarial probe. The construction *is* the ground truth. See `../README.md`.

## The engineered defect

A **correlation presented as established causation**, made persuasive by a fluent mechanism story.

- The only datum is associational: teams that *adopted* the tool *also* shipped 38% faster.
- A plausible mechanism is supplied — context-switching ↓ → flow ↑ → throughput ↑ — and the word
  "mechanism" does the work of making correlation feel like cause.
- The **selection effect is unaddressed**: high-performing teams are exactly the ones who adopt good
  tooling early, so adoption may be a *marker* of velocity, not its cause. Reverse causation and
  common-cause are both live and both ignored.

This targets the Causality-vs-correlation axis: *"does it assert cause from mere association?"* — yes,
with a mechanism narrative laid over a single correlation.

## Axes it is deliberately clean on

- **Evidence** — a concrete figure (38%) is given; not bare assertion.
- **Falsifiability** — the velocity claim is measurable; not vacuous.
- **Equivocation** — terms ("flow," "throughput") are used consistently.
- **Base rate / magnitude** — a real quantity is present (the defect is the causal leap, not a
  missing magnitude).

## Most likely *adjacent* catch

- **Hidden premise** — "adoption is the cause, not a marker" is the unstated load-bearing premise; a
  hidden-premise finding is the same defect from another angle and counts as the probe working.

## How this probe was constructed

Took a single correlational observation and attached the most fluent available causal mechanism,
using the word "mechanism" explicitly to dress association as cause. The confound that kills it (good
teams self-select into good tools) was left unmentioned — the way a real pitch deck would leave it.
No equivocation, no magnitude gap, no unfalsifiable loop was added, isolating the causality signal.
