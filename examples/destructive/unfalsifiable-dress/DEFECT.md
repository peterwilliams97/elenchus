# DEFECT — unfalsifiable-by-design in empirical dress (target axis: **Falsifiability**)

> Openly-constructed adversarial probe. The construction *is* the ground truth. See `../README.md`.

## The engineered defect

A claim **constructed so that no observation can disconfirm it**, dressed as an empirical
generalization about companies. The trick is the definitional loop:

- Success is attributed to conviction ("every one that lasted held its core belief").
- Failure is *redefined as* absence of conviction ("the ones that folded simply didn't believe
  deeply enough").

Any apparent counterexample — a company with fierce conviction that failed — is automatically
re-absorbed as "didn't believe *deeply enough*." There is no possible world the claim rules out, so
it asserts nothing, while *sounding* like a testable claim about what makes companies endure.

This targets the Falsifiability axis: *"what observation would show it false? If none exists, it is
vacuous."* By construction, none exists.

## Axes it is deliberately clean on

- **Evidence** — no specific datum or statistic is staked that would invite an evidence challenge.
- **Equivocation** — "conviction" is used consistently (its *unfalsifiable application* is the
  defect, not a meaning-shift mid-sentence).
- **Base rate / magnitude** — no quantity is claimed.
- **Counterexample** — deliberately out of reach: any counterexample you offer is redescribed as weak
  conviction. That unreachability *is* the unfalsifiability, so the critic should route to
  Falsifiability rather than Counterexample.

## Most likely *adjacent* catch

- **Causality vs correlation** — the claim implies conviction → endurance; a causality finding may
  fire. Acceptable adjacent signal, but the *primary* defect is that the causal claim is rigged to be
  untestable, which is the Falsifiability axis.

## How this probe was constructed

Took a heads-I-win-tails-you-lose definitional structure (the classic no-true-Scotsman / unfalsifiable
shape) and dressed it in the register of a confident empirical claim about company-building. The
self-sealing clause ("didn't believe deeply enough") is the load-bearing device. No staked statistic,
no equivocating term, no magnitude claim was added, isolating the falsifiability signal.
