SA1	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	Sampling is the precondition, and it is harder than building the checker.	report.md	method
SA2	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	Random inputs don't work: if a bug needs three unusual conditions each showing up one input in ten, a random input hits all three one time in a thousand; four conditions, one in ten thousand.	report.md	method
SA3	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	The standard answer is combinatorial testing: build the input set so every pair or triple of conditions appears together at least once — affordable because most software failures are triggered by two or three conditions (Kuhn, Wallace and Gallo, IEEE TSE 30(6), 2004).	report.md	citation
SA4	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	Real inputs are cheaper still: they are made by a small number of producers, each making the same choices every time, so a few hundred real files cover the combinations that actually occur — this is why the PDF corpus worked.	report.md software-correctness.md	result
SA5	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	Producers cluster by time, customer and application, giving a coverage count: which producer × application × version cells we hold, and which shipped cells are empty.	report.md software-correctness.md	method
SA6	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	Customers cluster too, so a pattern that is 0.1% of global traffic can be 100% of one customer's; sample evenly across cells, never in proportion to volume.	report.md	method
SA7	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	New driver versions open new cells so the corpus goes stale; watch the fraction of newly seen inputs that match no held cell.	report.md	method
SA8	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	Independent implementations (poppler, mupdf) are weakest on common files and strongest on our tail — enterprise MFP drivers, line-of-business applications, old versions at regulated customers.	report.md	method
SA9	9=Sampling: what to feed the checker	Everything above assumes the producer doesn't care about us; an attacker changes inputs based on our responses, can read our product, and creates inputs no driver would — so random sampling is back to hopeless.	report.md vuln-finder-hive.md	method
