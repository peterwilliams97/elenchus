CONTEXT
=======
The Meridian Transit Authority (MTA-North) operates a regional light-rail network across four
municipalities: Aldergrove, Brentmoor, Calderon, and Dunmore. The network opened its first line,
the Green Line, in 1998, connecting Aldergrove Central to Brentmoor Junction over 14 stations and
21.6 kilometres of track. A second line, the Amber Line, opened in 2006 and added 9 stations
between Brentmoor Junction and Calderon Docks. The most recent addition, the Slate Line, entered
revenue service in March 2019 and runs from Calderon Docks to Dunmore Heights across 11 stations.

Rolling stock is drawn from two fleets. The original fleet, designated Series 40, comprises 32
three-car units built by Kessler Rail between 1996 and 1999. Each Series 40 unit seats 96
passengers and has a maximum service speed of 80 km/h. Beginning in 2017, MTA-North took delivery
of the Series 70 fleet: 24 four-car units built by Ostmann Traction, each seating 140 passengers
with a maximum service speed of 100 km/h and regenerative braking that returns roughly 18 percent
of traction energy to the supply. The Series 70 units are assigned exclusively to the Slate Line
and to peak-hour Amber Line services; the Green Line runs Series 40 units at all times.

Fares are zone-based. The network is divided into three fare zones: Zone 1 covers Aldergrove and
Brentmoor, Zone 2 covers Calderon, and Zone 3 covers Dunmore. A single adult journey within one
zone costs 2.40 credits; a two-zone journey costs 3.60 credits; a three-zone journey costs 4.80
credits. Concession fares — available to riders under 18, riders over 65, and registered students
— are set at 50 percent of the adult fare, rounded down to the nearest 0.10 credit. A daily cap of
9.60 credits applies to adult fares regardless of the number of journeys; the concession daily cap
is 4.80 credits. Contactless bank cards and the reloadable MeridianGo smartcard are both accepted;
paper tickets were withdrawn in January 2021.

Service frequency varies by line and time of day. On weekdays, the Green Line runs every 6 minutes
during peak periods (07:00–09:30 and 16:30–18:30) and every 12 minutes off-peak. The Amber Line
runs every 10 minutes at peak and every 15 minutes off-peak. The Slate Line, the least busy, runs
every 15 minutes at peak and every 20 minutes off-peak. All three lines share a common operating
span of 05:15 to 00:30, except on Sundays, when service begins at 06:30. There is no overnight
service on any line.

Depot and maintenance facilities are concentrated at two sites. The Brentmoor Depot, adjacent to
Brentmoor Junction, houses the Series 40 fleet and performs light maintenance and daily cleaning.
Heavy overhaul and the Series 70 fleet are based at the Dunmore Works, which opened alongside the
Slate Line in 2019 and includes a wheel lathe, a bogie drop, and an automated train wash. MTA-North
targets a fleet availability of 92 percent each morning peak; in the 2022 financial year it reported
an average morning availability of 93.4 percent, its best result on record.

Governance sits with a seven-member board appointed by the four municipal councils, with Aldergrove
and Brentmoor each appointing two members and Calderon and Dunmore each appointing one; the seventh
member is an independent chair nominated by the state transport ministry. The board meets monthly.
Operating revenue in FY2022 was 84.2 million credits, of which 61 percent came from fares and the
remainder from a municipal operating subsidy and advertising concessions. Patronage in FY2022 was
41.7 million journeys, a recovery to 88 percent of the pre-2020 peak of 47.4 million.

Accessibility works completed in 2020 brought every station to step-free status except for two
Green Line stations, Aldergrove West and Millfield, where platform-to-street lifts are scheduled
for installation but not yet funded. All Series 70 units are fully wheelchair accessible with
level boarding; Series 40 units require a manual ramp deployed by the conductor.
